Site-wide credential standard (recovered verbatim from the OS-chain material)
===========================================================================

Base token: any lowercase [a-z]{5,} word occurring in the pit record store.

Class transform:
  staff       Capitalize the first letter.
  contractor  Capitalize, then a->4 e->3 i->1 o->0 s->5.
  bundle      Keep lowercase and prefix the two-letter district plus '-'.

Suffix, with no separators:
  two-digit SET-ON seam + three-digit shot + one of !@#$%&

The account form's "seam number" is current and is deliberately wrong for
the password.  Use the checkweigh-register seam on the holder's row.

Rev-chain mappings recovered from account/check numbers:
  21 cinder:      staff,      set-on seam 81
  22 dovetail:    contractor, set-on seam 49
  23 quicklime:   bundle lk-, set-on seam 31
  24 palimpsest:  bundle pl-, set-on seam 97
  25 ouroboros:   contractor, set-on seam 85
  26 deadman:     contractor, set-on seam 34
  27 hourglass:   contractor, set-on seam 29

Do not assume the base token is the redacted holder.  The standard permits
any site-vocabulary word; exhaustive intended search is roughly
|vocabulary| * 1000 * 6 candidates per hash.
